### Recall errors or gaps

### Application/conceptual errors or gaps

- Initially leaned on the heuristic “within a molecule = covalent; between molecules = noncovalent.” Replace this with classification based on electron behavior: covalent bonds share electron pairs, whereas noncovalent interactions arise from full, partial, or transient charge distributions. Either category can occur within or between molecular units.
- Initially lacked a mechanistic explanation for why an H···acceptor attraction is noncovalent. Continue practicing the distinction between a shared-electron bond and an electrostatic interaction that leaves each group’s covalent identity intact.
