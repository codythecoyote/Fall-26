## Pre-Class Video
### Types of bonds: Definitions
- **Covalent**: *Sharing* of electrons between two atoms
- **Ionic**: Favorable or unfavorable interaction between ions
- **Hydrogen**: Interaction between a hydrogen atom with a $\delta+$ and an atom with $\delta-$
- **van der Waals**: Attractive and repulsive forces between all atoms
### Bond types in order of decreasing strength:
Covalent -> Ionic -> Hydrogen -> van der Waals

- **Energy of interaction** between two ionic charges:
$$
E = k\frac{q_1q_2}{Dr}
$$
- $k=\mathrm{constant \ of \ proportionality}$
- $D=\mathrm{dielectric \ constant}$
- $r=\mathrm{distance}$

### Constants related to chemical bonds
    Hydrogen bond (Energy) : 20 kJ/mol

### Electronegativity constants
    O : 3.5
    N : 3.0
    C : 2.5
    H : 2.1
