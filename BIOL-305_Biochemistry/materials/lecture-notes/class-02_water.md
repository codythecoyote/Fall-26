# Biochemistry Class 2 - Water
2026-09-02

## Preclass Video
A water molecule has **4 regions** of partial charge, allowing it to make up to **4 hydrogen bonds** with other molecules
    
    H - O - H
    
- One $\delta+$ on each H
- Two $\delta-$ on O (one per lone pair)
- **ice** -> essentially all possible hydrogen bonds formed between water molecules -> highly ordered, static network
- **liquid form** -> some hydrogen bonds formed between water molecules -> less ordered, more dynamic network
