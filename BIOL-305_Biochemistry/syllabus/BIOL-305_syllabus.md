Course Goals

Describe the structure and function of biomolecules (particularly proteins, carbohydrates, lipids) and evaluate the relationship between molecular structure and function.
Describe the purpose and general procedure for common biochemical experimental techniques, select an appropriate approach for a specific goal, and interpret or predict results.
Conceptually and quantitatively evaluate the molecular basis of the function of biomolecules including binding interactions, enzyme activity, and regulation.
Evaluate and discuss common themes and specific processes in metabolism including regulation of metabolic processes at the cellular and organismal level.

Course Structure

The course is divided into 6 modules that each consist of 2-5 classes.  The learning objectives are a guide for what you should get out of each module. For each class, there may be pre-work including videos and a pre-class assignment which are very important to complete before class because the familiarity with the content is necessary for understanding what will happen during class sessions. The classes will reinforce or expand on the material in the videos, but the content in the videos may not be repeated in class. Class materials (such as PowerPoint slides) will be available on Canvas in advance and final versions will be posted after class. Class sessions will be interactive and may include clicker questions, group discussions, in-class work on worksheets, or other learning activities. Class sessions will be recorded, but please keep in mind that technical difficulties may occur and not all aspects of the course may be recorded. Collaborative assignments and exams will assess learning of the course content. Students will have the opportunity to communicate with the instructors, teaching assistants, and learning assistants in class as well as during student office hours and on Piazza (class discussion board).
